    Breathwork
    1. Set up. Lie on your back, knees bent, pillow under head and knees. Once it's easy, practice sitting, then standing.
    2. Hands. One on your upper chest, one just below your ribcage.
    3. In through the nose, ~4 sec. The lower hand rises; belly and lower ribs widen in every direction, including sides and back. The top hand stays nearly still.
    4. Let the floor drop. On the inhale, let the muscles between your legs soften and lower, like they're filling too. This is the link that matters.
    5. Out through the nose, ~6 sec. Belly falls. Don't force it. The long exhale is what calms things down.
    6. Repeat for 10 breaths.

    Can't feel the belly move? Lace your fingers behind your head. That locks the chest and makes you use the diaphragm.

    Avoid: pushing the belly out with your abs, lifting your shoulders or chest, and gulping air. If you get lightheaded, go smaller and slower.

    Core holds

    These are the floor muscles, the ones you'd use to stop mid-stream. Don't practice by actually stopping mid-stream; that's just for finding them.

    1. Squeeze and lift for 3–5 seconds. Keep glutes, thighs and abs relaxed, and keep breathing.
    2. Fully release for the same count. The release matters as much as the squeeze: a floor that stays tight tends to make you finish sooner.
    3. 10 reps per session · 2 sessions a day · 3 days a week.

    In the moment, you'll mostly use the release, letting go of tension on an exhale.

    The intensity scale
    • 1–4: warming up.
    • 5–6: cruising. This is where you want to spend time.
    • 7: yellow zone. Hit the brake here.
    • 8: close. Brake hard.
    • 9: redline. The point of no return. Too late to brake.
    • 10: finish.

    The main skill is learning what a 6, 7 and 8 feel like for you, so you can come down from 7–8 before you hit 9.

    The brake
    1. Stop, or slow way down in later phases.
    2. 3–4 belly breaths, with a long exhale.
    3. Release the floor. Let it drop and soften.
    4. Wait until you're back around a 4, then resume.

    Each brake counts as one "pause" in your log.
